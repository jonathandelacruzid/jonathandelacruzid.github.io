The challenge

Accountability that holds up on paper.

The organization needed an accountability program built around how it actually operates. The risk sat in documentation: incomplete records, employees placed incorrectly on the accountability matrix, and disciplinary actions unsupported by documentation all created legal and HR exposure. Under the direction of the Director of Talent Development, the Senior Instructional Designer and I translated those requirements into a program designed for 80–90 people leaders (anyone who supervises others).

The program

Two months.
80–90 leaders.

  1. Hybrid, full-day sessions

    Delivered over two months through eight-hour sessions two to three times a week, each training a cohort until all 80–90 leaders had completed the program. In-person sessions were paired with virtual sessions for leaders in out-of-state offices, extending consistent training across every location.

  2. Scenario-based games

    Game-based scenarios had leaders apply accountability decisions to realistic workplace situations, building judgment through applied practice.

  3. Scenario dialogue

    Facilitated, back-and-forth dialogue sessions let leaders rehearse the accountability conversations they are expected to hold.

  4. Companion workbook

    A workbook aligned to each presentation built in structured reflection and carried a single employee case through the full program, reinforcing continuity from session to session.

  5. The accountability matrix

    Leaders were introduced to the accountability matrix and revisited it in depth, establishing a shared framework for how and when employees move through it.

What I built

Participant workbook

A workbook mapped to each presentation, combining guided reflection with a continuous employee case to anchor learning from start to finish.

Slide decks

Presentation decks for the two-month program, structured around scenario games and dialogue practice to keep every session applied and interactive.

Facilitator guide structure

I defined the structure of the facilitator guide, which the Senior Instructional Designer developed, and conducted quality audits to ensure accuracy and consistency.

Copilot documentation agent

I designed, built and presented an AI agent in Microsoft Copilot that gives leaders an on-demand partner for practicing documentation.

The AI documentation coach

Practice to a five

A practice partner

Leaders draft documentation and refine it with the agent, a structured practice partner built in Microsoft Copilot.

1 → 5

Each draft is rated on a 1–5 scale against a clear target: revise until the documentation reaches a 5.

Org-wide

Deployed across the entire organization, strengthening leaders’ and HR’s grounding in legally sound documentation.

The business impact

What changed

Complete records

More complete documentation now reaches HR, so accountability decisions rest on a stronger record.

Matrix clarity

Leaders gained a clearer understanding of the accountability matrix and the considerations to weigh before moving an employee through it.

HR partnership

Leaders take more pause to partner with HR before acting on accountability decisions.
