Assistiv
Onboarding

Moving an AI-assisted LMS from passive video tutorials to interactive walkthroughs clients click through inside the product.

The challenge

Watching a walkthrough isn’t the same as doing it.

Assistiv is an AI-assisted learning management system. As it rolled out automated course generation, AI document drafting and dynamic quiz building, customer education still ran on Camtasia video walkthroughs: clear, but passive. Clients had to watch, pause, switch tabs and repeat the steps on their own, which added friction and cognitive load and slowed feature adoption.

The pivot

From watching
to doing.

  1. Start from video

    A Camtasia library on YouTube documented each AI feature and laid the groundwork.

  2. Rebuild as click-throughs

    Core workflows moved into Arcade, so clients click through the real fields, modals and navigation at their own pace.

  3. Embed where clients work

    The demos live on the Assistiv Support Page instead of sending clients to an external video platform.

  4. Update in place

    When the UI or an AI workflow changes, a demo is edited step by step, with no re-recording of voiceovers or video timelines.

The business impact

Measurable gains

+15%

Platform usage rate by Q2, as clients moved to hands-on interactive guides.

Fewer tickets

Clients completed bulk CSV user imports and curriculum generation on the first try, without contacting support.

Confident clients

Administrators and instructors reported navigating Assistiv’s AI capabilities independently.
