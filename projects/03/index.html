Scaling
Assistiv

From an AI literacy course that won early trust to a platform-native, AI-powered Support Hub serving 60–80 institutions.

The early challenge

Skepticism came before onboarding.

When Assistiv, an AI-assisted learning management system, launched with 5–10 active contracts, the obstacle wasn’t technical. Institutions and educators were wary of generative AI — data privacy, hallucinations, academic integrity. To build credibility we authored Interactive AI Overview, a web-based foundation course that turned abstract AI concepts into concrete instructional workflows. It reached hundreds of internal and prospective users and became a fixture of sales and product demos.

The inflection point

5–10 to
60–80.

  1. Sevenfold growth

    The educational push worked: Assistiv grew from 5–10 to 60–80 active contracts in a compressed timeframe.

  2. Questions turned tactical

    Clients moved past “What is generative AI?” to “How do we bulk-upload user CSVs?” and “How do I export class transcripts?”

  3. Support strained

    With dozens of institutions onboarding at once, manual support inquiries surged.

  4. The course retired

    Its job of building baseline AI literacy done, the foundation module was retired so resources could pivot to operational support.

Execution & leadership

End-to-end ownership

I owned delivery of the platform-native Support Hub from initiation to launch, managing timeline deliverables, instructional architecture and product alignment.

Intern mentorship

I supervised an instructional design intern across the full lifecycle, translating broad requirements into actionable design sprints. I provided clear structure and foundational frameworks, served as the go-to resource for questions, and coached cross-functional communication along the way — equipping the intern to lead testing and contribute directly to the production build.

Rapid prototyping in Lovable

I directed a structured prototyping workflow in Lovable. The intern led testing, while I provided oversight and quality assurance on every wireframe and UI iteration. Validated responsive layouts moved to the UX/UI designer for design QA, then directly to development.

Oversight to completion

I partnered with the UX/UI designer, development and adjacent teams to validate the build — ensuring interface consistency, accessibility compliance and seamless integration with Assistiv’s back-end AI before it shipped to production.

The business impact

Built to last

7× growth

The AI literacy course built the early trust behind Assistiv’s growth from 5–10 to 60–80 active contracts.

Fewer tickets

The Support Hub’s embedded AI assistant resolves operational questions in real time, deflecting routine onboarding tickets across 60–80 institutions.

On schedule

AI-native prototyping in Lovable plus structured design mentorship delivered an enterprise-ready support system on time.
